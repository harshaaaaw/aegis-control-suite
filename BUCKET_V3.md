## Detail

### 1. AGENTGATE — Agent Certification Gate
- KW 443/496 (89.4%) | Uniqueness 8.8 | Individuality 9.0 | Composite 8.91
- **Objective:** CI/CD for agents: integration-contract tests + sandbox replays + eval thresholds + governance checks must PASS before an agent ships. Owns: 'no untested agent reaches customers.'
- **Evidence:** Point tools all exist (Arize obs, promptfoo evals+GH action, Humanly/UserTrace synthetic users, Rift/gpt-drift drift) but NOBODY composes them into the ship/no-ship GATE + certificate. Gartner >40% agent projects canceled by 2027; Korea funded a $34M state testbed for this exact gap.

### 2. PROCUREIQ — AI Vendor Contract Intelligence
- KW 424/496 (85.5%) | Uniqueness 8.5 | Individuality 8.5 | Composite 8.52
- **Objective:** Reads AI vendor contracts, flags repricing/credit-redefinition traps, benchmarks rates vs peers, drafts negotiation positions pre-renewal. Owns: 'never surprise-billed again.'
- **Evidence:** NPI/SpendHound: AI spend +108% YoY, 85% miss forecasts, contracts renegotiated yearly. Runtime FinOps taken (TokenJam, Behest); CONTRACT side is consultant-only turf, no product found.

### 3. SWAPWATCH — Model Identity & Silent-Swap Sentinel
- KW 419/496 (84.6%) | Uniqueness 8.2 | Individuality 8.8 | Composite 8.49
- **Objective:** Continuously fingerprints every model endpoint you pay for; alerts when the thing behind the alias changes; produces before/after behavior and cost evidence for renegotiation. Owns: 'we always know what we are actually buying.'
- **Evidence:** OSS probes exist (gpt-drift, Rift Observatory, Codeform canary, llm-provider-audit) but all are dev CLIs; no enterprise product with fleet dashboards, alerting, procurement-grade $/correct reports. Providers swap models behind stable aliases without notice - every buyer exposed.

### 4. TWINTRUTH — Digital Twin Fidelity & ROI Validator
- KW 419/496 (84.5%) | Uniqueness 7.6 | Individuality 8.5 | Composite 8.18
- **Objective:** Measures twin-vs-reality drift continuously, certifies twin outputs, models business case BEFORE build. Owns: 'twins stay true and prove their worth.'
- **Evidence:** Fragmented niche OSS (STAMM soft-sensors, GameDriver game-engine twins); consulting-built twins (LTTS). No cross-industry productized drift validator + pre-build ROI modeler. Research: drift is #1 twin failure; ROI prediction is buyers' top blocker.

### 5. CAUSALA — Warehouse-Native Causal Decision Engine
- KW 414/496 (83.5%) | Uniqueness 7.0 | Individuality 8.5 | Composite 7.95
- **Objective:** Causal analysis inside Snowflake/BigQuery: which lever actually moved revenue, per segment, with confidence bounds. Owns: 'decisions cite causes, not correlations.'
- **Evidence:** causaLens ($51M) pivoted to digital workers; RootCause.ai serves giant enterprises only; Argenta is a solo repo. Mid-market warehouse-native causal ML thin.

### 6. SIMFACTORY — Enterprise RL Environments & Eval Factory
- KW 420/496 (84.8%) | Uniqueness 7.2 | Individuality 8.0 | Composite 7.89

- **Evidence:** Prime Intellect ($130M@$1B) proves demand for agent-training infra but is hosted/frontier-focused. Synthetic-data pure plays consolidated away. Self-hosted factory turning YOUR workflows into training environments: open.

### 7. KNOWPERMIT — Permissioned Institutional Memory
- KW 418/496 (84.2%) | Uniqueness 6.5 | Individuality 8.0 | Composite 7.64

- **Evidence:** API layer crowded (Mem0 55k stars/AWS exclusive, Letta, Zep, Modus). Surviving wedge: role-scoped memory serving HUMANS + agents with permission-aware retrieval.
